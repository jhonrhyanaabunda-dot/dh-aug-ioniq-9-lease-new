03The lease math

How an IONIQ 9 lease payment is built.

Every IONIQ 9 lease quote is assembled from a handful of moving parts. When you understand them before you sit down, the conversation goes faster and you can spot whether a deal is fair.

  • Capitalized cost (the price you're leasing on). This is the negotiated selling price of the car, not the sticker. We pull current inventory and start here. A lower cap cost lowers your payment more than almost anything else, which is why people who only negotiate the monthly payment often overpay.
  • Residual value. This is what the bank predicts the IONIQ 9 will be worth at lease-end, expressed as a percentage of MSRP. You pay for the difference between cap cost and residual, spread across your term. A higher residual means a lower payment. EVs have historically had volatile residuals, so this number moves, we'll show you the current one.
  • Money factor. This is the lease equivalent of an interest rate, tied to your approved credit tier. Multiply it by 2,400 to get a rough APR equivalent. Your credit profile sets this, which is why we run credit before quoting a firm payment.
  • Term length. Typical leases run 24, 36, or 39 months. Shorter terms usually mean a higher monthly but less total commitment; longer terms spread cost out. We'll show you 36 and 39 side by side so you can see the trade.
  • Due at signing. More money up front lowers the monthly payment; putting little or nothing down keeps cash in your pocket now. There's no single "right" answer, it depends on whether you'd rather protect cash flow or shave the monthly.
  • Annual mileage. Standard leases run 10,000 to 15,000 miles a year. Go over your allowance and you'll pay a per-mile penalty at turn-in. Tell us your real commute and we'll size the lease so you're not buying miles you won't drive.
  • Incentives and EV credits. Lease deals often route manufacturer incentives through the leasing bank. Federal EV tax credit eligibility for leased vehicles is a moving target, the rules are set by the IRS and the Treasury, and you can confirm current eligibility at irs.gov. We'll tell you what's being applied to your deal, in writing, rather than promising a credit that may not survive to signing.

04Lease vs. finance

Lease or finance the IONIQ 9, which is right for you?

Both are good options. The right one depends on how you drive, how long you keep a vehicle, and, for an EV specifically, how you feel about battery technology two and three years from now.

LeaseLower monthly

Lease the IONIQ 9 if you

Want a lower monthly payment than financing the same trim · like driving a new EV every two to three years · drive a predictable number of miles each year · want to stay under factory warranty the entire time · want to ride out the fast pace of EV improvement and step into the next IONIQ 9 at lease-end.

FinanceBuild equity

Finance the IONIQ 9 if you

Want to own the vehicle outright and build equity · plan to keep it well past the loan term · drive high or unpredictable mileage (no mileage cap, no penalty) · want the freedom to modify, sell privately, or trade on your own timeline.

Here's where we'll steer some people away from leasing. If you don't drive a predictable amount of miles each year, leasing an IONIQ 9 might be the wrong call.

  • High-mileage drivers, think twice. We've sat down with rideshare drivers and big-commute customers, looked at their real annual mileage, and told them straight up that a lease's overage penalties would eat any monthly savings alive.
  • At 25,000 miles a year, financing almost always wins.
  • We'd rather lose the lease and keep the customer than put someone in a deal that punishes them at turn-in.

05Before delivery

What we do before an IONIQ 9 goes to a lease customer.

A lease is a multi-year relationship, and the condition of the car at delivery matters more on a lease than almost anywhere else, because you're responsible for returning it in good shape.

Here's what our team does in-house, not as marketing copy but as routine process:

  • We inspect every IONIQ 9 in pre-delivery. Our techs verify the high-voltage battery state of health, confirm software is current, and road-test the car before it's handed over. We've declined to deliver cars that needed a software update first, better a one-day wait than a frustrated owner.
  • We verify charging hardware and the included equipment. We confirm the portable charging cable and adapters are in the car and that the charge port functions on both AC and DC, because nothing sours a new EV faster than getting home and discovering a missing accessory.
  • We walk you through home charging realistically. For a San Diego household, we'll talk through whether a standard 120V outlet is enough for your commute or whether a 240V Level 2 install makes sense. We've told plenty of low-mileage drivers they don't need to spend on a home charger at all, which isn't the upsell answer, but it's the honest one.
  • We pull a clean payoff and registration estimate up front. California EV registration and fees differ from gas vehicles, and you can confirm current fee schedules at dmv.ca.gov. We'd rather you see the all-in number before signing than be surprised at the counter.

06Ownership costs

Ownership costs San Diego drivers should plan for.

EVs change the math on running costs, and it helps to plan around the real categories rather than guesses.

Charging vs. fuel

Charging an IONIQ 9 at home overnight on California residential rates is typically far cheaper per mile than gasoline, though San Diego's electricity rates run higher than the national average, worth checking your specific utility plan. Public DC fast charging costs more than home charging but buys you speed on road trips.

Maintenance

EVs skip oil changes, but they still need tire rotations, cabin filters, brake fluid, and the occasional alignment. That's where our service program earns its keep.

Tires

EV torque and weight wear tires faster than many drivers expect. This is exactly why the complimentary tire check on every visit matters, catching uneven wear early saves a set of tires.

Insurance

EVs can carry different premiums; get a quote before you commit so there are no surprises.

For independent third-party context on IONIQ 9 reliability, owner reviews, and projected resale, drivers we talk to often cross-reference Edmunds and Kelley Blue Book before they come in. We encourage it, a more informed customer is an easier customer to help.
